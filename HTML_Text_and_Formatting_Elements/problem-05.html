SCIENCE NOTES

Physics

1. Newton's First Law: An object at rest stays at rest and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and in the same direction unless acted upon by an unbalanced force.
2. Newton's Second Law: The acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to its mass.
3. Newton's Third Law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.
4. Ohm's Law: V = I * R, where V is voltage, I is current, and R is resistance.
5. Law of Conservation of Energy: Energy cannot be created or destroyed; it can only be transformed from one form to another.
6. Some Physics Formulas: F = m * a , E = m * c2 , P = W / t (power) kinetic energy = (1/2)mv2, potential energy = mgh

Chemistry

1. Atomic Structure: Atoms consist of a containing protons and neutrons, surrounded by electrons in energy levels.
2. Chemical Bonding: Atoms bond together to form molecules through ionic, covalent, or metallic bonds.
3. Chemical Reactions: Reactants are transformed into products through chemical reactions, which can be classified as synthesis, decomposition, single replacement, or double replacement.
4. Periodic Table: Elements are organized based on their atomic number and properties in the periodic table.
5. Laws of Chemistry: The law of conservation of mass states that mass is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.
6. Some Chemical Formulas: H2O (water), CO2 (carbon dioxide), C6H12O6 (glucose)

Biology

1. Cell Theory: All living organisms are composed of cells, which are the basic unit of life.
2. Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium: p2+2pq+q2=1
3.Gametes & Genotypes: Number of gametes = 2n, phenotypes = 3n, genotypes = 2n where n = number of heterozygous gene pairs.
4. Photosynthesis: 6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy → C6H12O6 + 6O2
5. Respiration: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + energy (ATP)
