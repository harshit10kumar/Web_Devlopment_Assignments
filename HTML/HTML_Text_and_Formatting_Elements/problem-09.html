STUDENT TERMS & RULES

General Rules

1. Students must maintain a minimum GPA of 2.0 to remain in good standing.

2. All students are expected to adhere to the code of conduct and academic integrity policies.p>

3. Students must attend all scheduled classes and complete assignments on time.

Attendance Policy

1. Students are allowed a maximum of 3 unexcused absences per semester.
2. Excused absences must be documented and approved by the instructor.
3. Students who exceed the allowed number of absences may be subject to academic probation or dismissal.

Examination Policy

1. Students must arrive on time for all examinations.
2. No electronic devices are allowed in the examination room.
3. Students who violate the examination policy may face disciplinary action.

Assignment Submission Policy

1. All assignments must be submitted by the specified deadline.
2. Late submissions may incur a penalty or may not be accepted at all.
3. Students are responsible for ensuring that their assignments are submitted correctly and on time.

Disciplinary Actions

1. Students who violate the terms and rules may face disciplinary actions, including warnings, probation, suspension, or expulsion.
2. The severity of the disciplinary action will depend on the nature and frequency of the violation.
3. Students have the right to appeal any disciplinary action taken against them.
