TechFest 2026 – Innovate, Create & Transform

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is the annual technology festival of our college, bringing together students, developers, innovators and technology enthusiasts. The event provides an exciting platform to learn, compete, collaborate and showcase technical skills.

Participants will get the opportunity to explore emerging technologies, attend interactive sessions and take part in exciting technical activities. The festival is designed to encourage creativity, teamwork and practical problem-solving.

Highlights

>💻 Coding and Programming Competition
>🤖 Robotics and AI Challenge
>🌐 Web Development Competition
>🎮 Gaming and Tech Quiz
>🚀 Startup and Innovation Showcase
>🎤 Expert Talks and Technical Workshops

Date & Venue

Date: 15–16 November 2026
Venue: Main Auditorium & Innovation Lab
GLA University, Mathura, Uttar Pradesh

Speakers

The festival will feature experienced technology professionals, entrepreneurs and industry experts who will share their knowledge about artificial intelligence, software development, cybersecurity and future technologies.

Attendees will also have the opportunity to interact with the speakers and learn about real-world career opportunities in the technology industry.
