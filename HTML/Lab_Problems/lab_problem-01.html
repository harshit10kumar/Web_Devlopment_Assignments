About university

GLA University, established in 1998 in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, is a leading private institution accredited with an 'A+' grade by NAAC. The university spans a sprawling campus offering a wide array of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, management, pharmacy, commerce, and computer applications. Known for its world-class infrastructure, GLA features modern laboratories, research centers, and digital libraries that promote high-quality academic growth. It also maintains a phenomenal placement track record, consistently connecting students with top-tier global companies like Microsoft, Amazon, Wipro, and TCS, alongside hosting vibrant campus fests and technical clubs for all-round development.

Admission Overview

GLA University welcomes students who are interested in pursuing undergraduate and postgraduate programs. The admission process includes checking eligibility, submitting an application, document verification and fee payment.

Eligibility

Applicants must meet the eligibility criteria specified for their selected course.

  • Educational Qualification: Must have passed the required qualifying examination.
  • Minimum Marks: Must satisfy the minimum marks requirement.
  • Entrance Examination: Applicable courses may require an entrance examination.
  • Valid Documents: All required documents must be submitted.

Application Process

Follow these steps in the given sequence:

  1. Visit the official university admission portal.
  2. Register using your name, email and mobile number.
  3. Fill in the online application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the application form.
  7. Complete counselling or admission formalities.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2027

Required Documents

The order of these documents does not matter:

  • 10th Mark Sheet
  • 12th Mark Sheet
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • Government-issued Identity Proof
  • Category Certificate, if applicable
  • Entrance Examination Score Card, if applicable

Campus facilites

GLA University provides a highly modern, self-contained 110+ acre campus equipped with comprehensive infrastructure to support academic, residential, and recreational needs.

Residential & Dining Facilities

* Hostels: Separate residential blocks for boys and girls accommodating over 9,000 students, featuring AC/Non-AC rooms, 24/7 power backup, and high-speed Wi-Fi.
* Mess & Cafeterias: Multiple hygienic mess halls serving nutritious vegetarian meals, alongside open-air cafeterias, food courts, and popular tuck shops.

Academic & Research Infrastructure

* Smart Classrooms: Over 120 amphitheater-style, air-conditioned lecture theatres equipped with interactive digital projectors and sound systems.
* Central Library: A massive, fully automated digital knowledge resource housing over 1,50,000 books, thousands of national/international journals, and e-learning portals.
* Laboratories: More than 150 advanced technical labs, including specialized centers setup in collaboration with industry giants like Automation Anywhere and Rockwell Automation.

Health, Fitness & Sports

* Sports Complex: Expansive outdoor fields for cricket, football, and hockey, alongside dedicated courts for basketball, volleyball, tennis, and a well-equipped indoor sports arena.
* Fitness Centers: Multi-facility, modern gymnasiums separate for boys and girls with certified fitness trainers.
* Medical Care: An in-campus dispensary and medical center with residential doctors, ambulances, and tie-ups with major local city hospitals for emergencies.

Campus Utilities & Security

* Banking: In-house branches of major national banks along with multiple 24/7 ATM counters spread across the campus.
* Transport: A robust fleet of university-owned deluxe buses connecting the campus to Mathura, Agra, Kosi Kalan, and nearby regions.
* Security: A secure environment managed by a professional security force, complete with comprehensive CCTV surveillance and biometric entry access points.
