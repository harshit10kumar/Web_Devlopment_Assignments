Welcome to Horizon University

Welcome to Horizon University, a student-focused institution committed to academic excellence, innovation, and social responsibility.

Our university provides students with high-quality education, practical learning opportunities, research facilities, and career guidance. Applications for the 2026-27 academic session are now open.

We encourage students to develop critical thinking, creativity, and professional skills.

Academic Programs

Bachelor of Computer Science

A four-year program covering programming, databases, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and software engineering.

Bachelor of Business Administration

A three-year program covering management, finance, marketing, entrepreneurship, and leadership.

Bachelor of Arts in Media and Communication

A three-year program focused on journalism, digital media, public relations, and communication.

Master of Data Science

A two-year postgraduate program covering data analysis, machine learning, statistics, and data visualization.

Application Process

  1. Review the program and eligibility requirements.
  2. Create your application profile.
  3. Complete the online application form.
  4. Upload academic and identity documents.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee.
  6. Submit the completed application.
  7. Check the portal for merit-list and admission updates.
