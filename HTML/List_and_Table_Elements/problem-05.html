Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026, a three-day celebration of creativity, talent, technology, sports and cultural activities. Students from different departments are invited to participate, compete and enjoy a memorable college experience.

Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest brings together students, faculty members and guests for a variety of academic, cultural and entertainment events. Participants can showcase their skills through individual and team competitions.

The fest will be conducted from 10 March 2026 to 12 March 2026 on the college campus.

"Our annual fest is an opportunity for every student to discover their talent, build friendships and create lasting memories. Participate enthusiastically and make Fest 2026 a grand success."
— Fest Organizer

Registration Process

Participants must complete their registration before the deadline to take part in the competitions.

Registration Deadline: 5 March 2026

  1. Visit the official fest registration page.
  2. Create an account using your college email address.
  3. Select the competition or competitions you want to enter.
  4. Enter the required participant or team details.
  5. Pay the applicable registration fee.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save the registration/reference number received by email.
  8. Report to the event venue at the scheduled time.

Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college identity card.
  • Participants must report at the venue at least 30 minutes before their event.
  • Registration fees, once paid, are non-refundable.
  • Participants must follow the instructions of event coordinators.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • Participants must maintain cleanliness on the college campus.
  • Use of abusive or offensive language is prohibited.
  • The decision of the judges will be considered final.

Fest Terms

Open Event
An event in which eligible participants from different colleges can participate.
Team Event
A competition in which two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
A competition in which a participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for organizing and managing a particular event.
Final Round
The concluding stage of a competition in which selected participants compete for the winning positions.
