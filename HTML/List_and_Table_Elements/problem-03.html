Sequence Before a Practical Class

  1. Read the practical instructions before entering the lab.
  2. Bring the required notebook and laboratory materials.
  3. Review the previous practical exercise.
  4. Reach the laboratory before the scheduled time.
  5. Follow the faculty's instructions before starting the experiment.

Academic Rules

  • Students must maintain at least 75% attendance.
  • Students must carry their identity cards on campus.
  • Mobile phones should be kept silent during lectures.
  • Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms and laboratories.
  • Assignments must be submitted before the specified deadline.
  • Students must follow laboratory safety instructions.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A classroom session focused on theoretical concepts and explanations.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or exercises.
Tutorial
A guided academic session used for problem solving and discussion.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, and activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity designed to apply theoretical knowledge.
