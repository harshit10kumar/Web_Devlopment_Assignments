General Buying Factors

  • Processor: Choose a processor suitable for your coursework and applications.
  • RAM: More RAM improves multitasking performance.
  • Storage: SSD storage provides faster boot and application loading.
  • Display: Consider screen size, resolution and panel quality.
  • Battery: A larger battery is useful for students who work away from power outlets.
  • Warranty: A longer warranty provides better protection for your investment.

How to Choose Your Laptop

  1. Determine your budget.
  2. Identify the software and applications you need to run.
  3. Choose an appropriate processor and amount of RAM.
  4. Select sufficient SSD storage for your files and applications.
  5. Compare display size, resolution and refresh rate.
  6. Check battery capacity and expected battery life.
  7. Compare warranty coverage and after-sales service.
  8. Compare the final prices and choose the laptop offering the best value.

Important Laptop Terms

RAM
Random Access Memory temporarily stores data used by running applications and helps with multitasking.
SSD
Solid State Drive is a fast storage device used to store the operating system, applications and personal files.
CPU
Central Processing Unit is the main processor responsible for executing instructions and performing calculations.
GPU
Graphics Processing Unit handles graphics and is especially important for gaming, video editing and 3D applications.
Refresh Rate
Refresh rate is the number of times a display updates its image per second, measured in Hertz (Hz).
Battery Capacity
Battery capacity indicates how much electrical energy a battery can store, commonly measured in watt-hours (Wh).
